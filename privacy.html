Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use CodeWix, we collect the following information:

  • Account Data: Your email address, display name, and authentication status (via Firebase).
  • User Content: The code you write, AI prompts you submit, and sites you publish.
  • Usage Data: Token usage, date of registration, and strike/ban history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the CodeWix platform.
  • Process your AI requests using our third-party AI provider (Groq).
  • Enforce our Terms of Service through automated moderation.
  • Process payments for Pro subscriptions.

3. Third-Party Services

We share necessary data with the following trusted third parties to operate our platform:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database hosting, and file storage.
  • Groq API: Your AI prompts are sent to Groq to generate responses and to scan for policy violations. Groq does not use your data to train their public models.
  • Selar: For secure payment processing (we do not store your credit card details).
  • Render: For server hosting.

4. Automated Moderation & AI Scanning

For the safety of our community, your public profile data (usernames, published site names/descriptions) and AI chat prompts are periodically scanned by an automated AI moderation system. This is to detect and prevent violations of our Terms of Service.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you may contact us at codewix@proton.me. Banned accounts may retain data to prevent circumvention of bans.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security measures (including secure token-based authentication and encrypted database connections) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at codewix@proton.me.
