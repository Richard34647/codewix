Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

CodeWix respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you consent to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Email address and password when you create an account
  • Code, files, and text you write inside the workspace
  • Messages you send to the AI assistant
  • Any communication you send us via the contact form

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • IP address (used for security and abuse prevention)
  • Pages visited and features used within CodeWix
  • Timestamps of account activity

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Process your requests to the AI assistant
  • Respond to support requests and questions
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Send important service-related announcements (not marketing, unless you opt in)

4. Third-Party Services

CodeWix relies on trusted third parties to operate:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — handles account creation and login. Your email and password hash are stored securely on Google's servers.
  • Groq — processes your AI assistant prompts. When you send a message to the AI, the content of that message (and the conversation history) is sent to Groq's API to generate a response.
  • Render — hosts our application servers. Requests to CodeWix pass through Render's infrastructure.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

CodeWix uses browser cookies and local storage to:

  • Keep you logged in between visits
  • Remember workspace preferences (selected AI model, editor state)
  • Maintain security tokens issued by Firebase

You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings, though doing so will log you out.

6. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to keep records for legal or security reasons.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential.

8. Children's Privacy

CodeWix is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise these rights, contact us via the Contact page.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
