Our Story

CodeWix started as a simple idea: what if learning to code felt less like reading a textbook and more like building something real? We built a workspace where beginners and experienced developers sit side by side — writing code, running it live, and asking an AI assistant for help whenever they get stuck.

Today, CodeWix is a learning platform, a sandbox IDE, and an AI pair-programmer all rolled into one. Whether you are writing your first console.log() or shipping a full-stack application, our tools adapt to your level.
